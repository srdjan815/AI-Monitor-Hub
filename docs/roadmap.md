# Roadmap

## Frozen Foundation

The `foundation-v1.0` release point covers Product Core, the Product Attribute
Platform, Product Content, Inventory, Execution, migrations, security,
observability, performance evidence, and regression tests.

## Next chapter

The recommended branch is `feature/supplier-platform`. Supplier design or
implementation is intentionally outside the Foundation closure sprint.

Later chapters may add Import, Matching, Pricing, ERP synchronization, AI,
Scraper, Media, and Publishing without moving their state into Catalog Product
or making Redis canonical domain storage.
